# About this project

**Full disclosure:** I have no background in electronics or software. But what I am full of is curiosity and keenness to learn. So I'm learning about electronics and software by solving one real problem: I am forgetful of my stuff, especially my keys. Instead of just buying a product from the internet, I thought: why don't I just build one? The best part is that I know nothing about how to build one. Hence this page, to document my journey from knowing nothing to building something that I would like to see come alive in the real world.

What I have realised is that I love to solve problems. The problem I am solving is *"How can I find my keys without having to turn my apartment upside down?"* To solve it, I have broken the journey into a set of smaller problems that I must solve. This way, I know where I am headed and how far I have come.

**Note:** This is a hobby. It will never be a product. Every choice favours *what teaches me most* and *what is simplest*.

## What I'm building

A small tracker that lives in a zipped pocket of a child's backpack:

- **Small and slim:** about the size of a credit card, ideally about 1 cm thick.
- **Trip mode:** sends its location every 30 to 60 seconds for a few hours, then charges overnight.
- **Location ladder:** uses the cheapest way to find itself first. A motion sensor wakes it, then it tries WiFi (works indoors), then GPS (outdoors), then cell towers (rough fallback).
- **Group tether:** the teacher carries a small Bluetooth beacon. If the tracker loses it for about 2 minutes, it raises an alarm.
- **Geofence:** an alert if the group leaves the planned destination area.

## How I'm learning

The whole project is a [list of problems](problems.html), solved one at a time, easiest first. The bar at the top shows how many are solved.

## Safety and privacy rules

- No coin-cell batteries, no loose small parts, a rigid enclosure closed with screws, never worn around the neck.
- Only a certified LiPo battery with a protection circuit.
- No microphone or listen-in feature.
- The tracking portal is private, behind a login.
- This website never shows my children's names, photos, locations, device or SIM numbers, keys or passwords.
